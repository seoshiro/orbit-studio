# Visual assets

`public/textures/earth-blue-marble.jpg` is the unmodified 2048 × 1024 NASA Blue Marble 2002 land surface, shallow water and shaded topography image (238,676 bytes).

- Download: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57752/land_shallow_topo_2048.jpg
- Source and credits: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/
- Credit: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; Reto Stöckli (land surface, shallow water), Robert Simmon (ocean color and compositing), MODIS teams and USGS EROS Data Center (topography).
- NASA image/media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

NASA makes this imagery freely available for educational and public use. This historical surface composite draws on observations from 2001; it is not live weather, telemetry, or evidence of NASA endorsement. There are no NASA logos in ORBIT. The application links the source under primary references in all three languages.

An original 1024 × 512 roughness map is computed once from the packaged image to distinguish ocean from land. An illustrative Fresnel atmosphere adds a faint limb. No extra texture requests, cloud animation, Earth spin dynamics or ground track are introduced.

Spacecraft geometry, irregular foil normal/roughness maps and solar-cell texture with busbars are original procedural assets. They are deterministic and shared across both scenes. Three.js and its geometry helpers are MIT licensed; Inter Variable is under SIL OFL 1.1.
